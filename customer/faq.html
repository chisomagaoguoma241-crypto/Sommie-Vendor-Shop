Does this checkout place a real order?

No. The checkout is a visual and interaction demonstration only. It does not collect payment information, send an order, or create a real fulfilment request.

Where are the bag and wishlist stored?

Your bag, wishlist, colour mode, and demo account information are stored in this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data will remove them.

Can I use the coupon code?

Enter SOMMIE10 in the bag to see the demonstration discount calculation. It has no commercial effect.

How do returns work in the demo?

The stated 30-day return window is a user-interface example. It does not start a real return or communicate with a merchant.

Why are there no star ratings or reviews?

This demonstration does not fabricate customer feedback. Verified reviews require a real collection and moderation workflow.
