Our lens

Useful objects. Honest materials. Design that rewards living with it over time.

A shop should feel like a point of view.

Sommie Vendor Shop is a browser-native concept store built around the belief that digital shopping can be more selective, more tactile, and more useful. The collection is designed to be small enough to navigate with curiosity and broad enough to reflect a complete, everyday life.

What makes the cut

We look for directness in form, clarity in function, and materials that become more interesting through use. The selection moves across personal style, the home, and the small objects that make a day work better.

Designed to be re-visited

Instead of endless rows of products, the edit evolves in measured drops. That gives each piece room to be understood, compared, saved, and considered before it goes into a bag.

A transparent demo

This project is a frontend-only prototype. Its basket, profile, and preference features run in the browser’s local storage; no account, payment, or order information is sent to a server.
