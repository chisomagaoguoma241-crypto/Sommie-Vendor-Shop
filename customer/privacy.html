Privacy policy

Local by design.

How this frontend demonstration treats the information stored in your browser.

Effective

15 August 2026

Summary

This prototype does not include a backend, database, payment provider, or email delivery service. It therefore does not transmit the demo data you enter to Sommie or any merchant service.

Browser storage

The website may store your bag contents, wishlist, selected colour mode, recently viewed pieces, and demonstration account details in local storage. This data stays on the device and browser profile where it was created.

Third-party visual resources

The interface loads selected image and font resources over the internet. Those providers may receive standard technical request information, such as an IP address, under their own policies.

Your control

You can clear local storage at any time through your browser settings. Doing so resets the interactive demo state, including any demonstration account and bag details.
