Illustrative delivery

Standard delivery is shown as complimentary for baskets above $150. A priority option is presented in checkout. Both are UI examples only and do not result in shipment.

Illustrative returns

The customer experience presents a 30-day consideration period. In a production store, this should be replaced with the merchant’s approved policy, legal terms, return instructions, and contact route.
